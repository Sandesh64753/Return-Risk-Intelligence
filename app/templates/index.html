Operational Decision Workflow

How order attributes flow from checkout ingestion to calibrated risk evaluation.

STEP 01

Order & Catalog Ingestion

Collect point-in-time order parameters available strictly before shipment: merchandise category, seller rating, defect rate, discount level, payment method, and transit distance.

STEP 02

Leakage-Safe Preprocessing

Exclude post-delivery variables (e.g. delivery delays, support calls). Apply verified transformations: log1p on catalog prices, median imputation, standard scaling, and one-hot encoding.

STEP 03

Calibrated Risk Classification

Inference runs against the balanced Logistic Regression model. Predictions are classified against the tuned 0.3974 threshold designed to capture approximately 90% of returns.

Key Capabilities for Fulfillment & Merchandising Teams

Packaging & Handling Reinforcement

Flag fragile merchandise dispatched over extended shipping distances or through seller fulfillment routes so operations can ensure appropriate protective packaging.

Targeted Payment Verification

Identify Cash-on-Delivery orders with elevated return probability to initiate automated address and availability confirmations prior to courier pickup.

Merchant Defect Monitoring

Detect orders pairing high supplier defect rates with low product review ratings, preventing recurring customer dissatisfaction and expensive return shipping.
